Motor ports

Choose which port drives each wheel

Hold a port's Run button to drive it at the chosen power; it stops the moment you let go. If a wheel turns backwards when driven forward, tick Invert. Never rewire a motor to fix direction.

Servo board

PCA9685

Angles map 0–180° onto 500–2500 µs. A held servo keeps its position until released. OE on GPIO4 switches all 16 outputs off in hardware; it is not a power cutoff.

Ramp servos

Channels and positions

Both ramp servos move together. A mirrored servo turns the opposite way so the two sides match. Save before testing.
